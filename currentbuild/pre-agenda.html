Agenda for the pre-meeting

Proposed agenda for the hackathon pre-Hackathon meeting on 2. November 2026.
NOTE! The agenda is currently under active development and changes will occur without further notice.

Intro

The goal of the pre-Hackathon meeting is to give a quick introduction to the different hackathon tracks, and make the participants capable of doing necessary preparations for the Hackathon. The pre-Hackathon meeting will be virtual only.

2. November 2026

The meeting will contain a short introduction. Each participant will present themselves and say what track they want to focus on. We will then split up in different tracks meetings (choose the one you are most interested in/want to start out with working on) and disuss track specific issues in the breakout sessions.
